Problem Statement and Business Context
In the FMCG sector, forecast inaccuracy translates directly into financial leakage—either through Inventory Carrying Costs (overstock) or Lost Sales/Service Level Failures (stockouts). This project addresses the following professional challenges:
●	Demand Volatility: Frequent promotions and market shifts that distort the baseline "true" demand.
●	The Bullwhip Effect: Amplified demand fluctuations as signals move from Traditional Trade kiosks to central warehouses.
●	Inventory Imbalance: Persistent stockouts on high-priority "A-products" (Revenue drivers) and wasteful overstocks on "C-products."
●	Siloed Intelligence: A lack of integrated Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) visibility across the 15 countries of operation.
Core Objectives:
1.	Quantify the drivers of demand (Price, Promotion, Seasonality).
2.	Improve forecast precision to optimize safety stock and service levels.
3.	Develop a scalable framework for cross-regional inventory management.
